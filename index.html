The love I Never Expected ❤️

Aarohi was the only daughter of her family, loved deeply by her two elder brothers. Among everyone, Rohan — her brother’s best friend — became special in her life. As she grew up, her feelings turned into love. One day, she confessed her love, but Rohan rejected her because she was still in high school. Heartbroken, Aarohi changed. Rohan went abroad. Years later, he returned — realizing he loved her. But now Aarohi had changed, and someone else, Aman, had entered her life. Rohan felt jealous and regretful. Aarohi was angry and distant. But love that is true never fades. In the end, Aarohi realized she still loved Rohan. And this time... he never let her go.

The Love That Waited ❤️

Meera was quiet and emotional, while Aditya was confident and lively. They became close friends in college and slowly fell in love—but neither confessed. One day, Aditya saw Meera with another boy, Raghav, and assumed she had moved on. Without asking, he distanced himself. Meera was confused and hurt by his sudden change. Later, she confronted him and the truth came out—Raghav was her cousin. Aditya realized his mistake: he chose assumptions over trust. He apologized sincerely, and this time, he didn’t walk away. Meera forgave him, and they finally accepted their love.

First Love ❤️

Naina and Kabir were best friends who talked every day—mostly through late-night calls. Kabir loved her but never confessed. Naina also had feelings, but she waited for him to say it first. One night, Kabir decided to confess. He called her again and again—but her phone was busy. That same night, Naina was on another call… with her parents. They were fixing her marriage. The next day, Kabir finally met her, ready to confess—but before he could speak, she said softly: “My marriage is fixed.” He smiled… hiding his heartbreak. Years later, they met again. Same feelings. Same silence. But this time, Kabir didn’t wait. “I love you,” he said. Naina smiled sadly, showing her ring. “Too late.”

When i fall in love with you ❤️

Rain had just stopped when Kiara ran into Reyansh at the old lakeside bridge. It had been three years since they last spoke — three years of silence, misunderstandings, and unfinished feelings. Back then, they were inseparable. They dreamed together, laughed together, and promised they would never drift apart. But life had other plans. A small misunderstanding slowly turned into distance, and distance turned into silence. That night, under the glowing lanterns beside the lake, neither of them knew what to say at first. Yet their eyes still carried the same warmth they once knew. “I used to come here hoping I’d see you again,” Reyansh admitted quietly. Kiara smiled sadly. “And I stopped coming because I knew I’d miss you more.” For a moment, the world around them felt still. No anger remained, only memories and feelings that never truly faded. As the moon reflected on the water, Reyansh held her hand gently and whispered, “Maybe some love stories pause… but they never really end.” ❤️
